How often should you deep clean your home?

A deep clean is a reset, not a bigger weekly tidy. Here is how to decide how often your home needs one and what to include.

Hannah ReyesNov 12, 2026 · 5 min read
Written by
Hannah ReyesFounder & Lead Cleaner

Share

Most homes get a weekly tidy. Fewer get a proper reset. A deep clean reaches everything the weekly routine skips, and a simple schedule means you never have to dread it.

What a deep clean actually covers

Regular cleaning keeps surfaces tidy: floors vacuumed, counters wiped, bathrooms refreshed. A deep clean goes further. It reaches behind and under furniture, inside appliances, along skirting boards and window tracks, and into the grout lines and vents that quietly collect dust and grease.

A simple rule of thumb

For most households, two to four deep cleans a year is the sweet spot. Where you land depends on how your home is used:

  • Quiet home, regular weekly cleaning: twice a year, ideally in spring and autumn.
  • Children or pets: every three months, as floors, soft furnishings and entrance areas work harder.
  • Allergies in the household: bedrooms and soft furnishings more often, as dust and pollen build up.
  • Life events: before or after moving, renovations, a new baby or hosting guests.
Tip. Tie deep cleans to the calendar. The first weekend of each season is easy to remember and spreads the work evenly across the year.

Room by room: what to prioritise

You do not need to do everything at once. If time is tight, start with the rooms that work hardest.

  • Kitchen: oven interior, fridge shelves, extractor filter and cabinet fronts.
  • Bathrooms: grout, shower head, extractor fan and the base of taps and fixtures.
  • Bedrooms: mattress and under the bed, curtains and the tops of wardrobes.
  • Living areas: sofa cushions, skirting boards and the space behind the TV unit.

A deep clean makes your weekly clean faster, because the hard work is already done.

Hannah Reyes, Founder & Lead Cleaner

Make the time between cleans easier

The best deep clean is the one you do not dread. Between visits, keep it light: wipe as you go, take ten minutes for an evening reset, and give one zone of the home a little extra attention each week. Small habits compound, and a deep clean then becomes a refresh rather than a rescue.
