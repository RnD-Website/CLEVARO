Pricing FAQ

Good to know.

More questions? Browse the full FAQ or call us on +1 (628) 555-0147.

Is there a contract?

No. Recurring plans can be paused, changed or cancelled at any time. We only ask for 24 hours’ notice before a visit.

How do the prices work for bigger homes?

Choose your size above to see the starting price. For very large or heavily lived-in homes we confirm a fixed price after a quick review.

When do I pay?

No payment is taken when you book. You pay after the clean, once you have seen the result.

What is included in a regular clean?

Dusting, vacuuming and mopping, kitchen and bathroom clean, bed making and a tidy of every room. See the full checklist on the services page.

Choose your service, pick a time and we will confirm a fixed price by email within two hours.
