01 — General

The basics

How Clevaro works and what to expect on the day.

How long does a visit take?

A regular clean of a two-bedroom home usually takes two to three hours. Deep cleans and move-out cleans take four to seven hours depending on size and condition. We give you a time window when we confirm.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients give us a key, a door code or a lockbox. If you prefer to be there, that is fine too. Either way you will get a message when we arrive and when we finish.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. We bring everything, including eco-safe, allergen-aware products and professional equipment. If you would like us to use something specific, add it to your booking notes.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Portland and the surrounding suburbs. Enter your address in the booking form and we will confirm coverage straight away.

Are your cleaners vetted and insured?

Every cleaner is background-checked, trained in our room-by-room method and covered by our liability insurance before their first visit.

02 — Booking

Booking & scheduling

Choosing a time, changing plans and recurring visits.

How do I book a cleaning?

Use the Book a Cleaning button on any page. Choose your service and home size, pick a date and time, and add your details. We confirm by email within two hours.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes. You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your visit. Recurring plans can be paused or changed at any time.

Can I get the same cleaner each time?

We aim to send the same small team to every recurring visit so they get to know your home. If you have a favourite, tell us and we will request them.

How far ahead should I book?

Most slots are available within a few days. Weekends and the end of the month fill up quickly, so book a week ahead if you need a specific day.

Do you offer same-day cleaning?

Sometimes. Call us on +1 (628) 555-0147 and we will check what is available that day.

03 — Pricing

Pricing & payment

How prices are set and when you pay.

How is the price worked out?

Prices start from the size of your home and the service you choose. We confirm a fixed price after a quick review of your space, so there are no surprises on the day.

When do I pay?

No payment is taken when you book. You pay after the clean, once you have seen the result.

Do recurring plans cost less?

Yes. Weekly visits save 15%, bi-weekly 10% and monthly 5% on every visit, and you can pause or cancel at any time.

Are there any extra or hidden fees?

No. The price we confirm is the price you pay. Add-ons such as inside the oven are listed on the pricing page and only added when you ask.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major cards and bank transfer. You will receive a receipt by email after every visit.

04 — Products & safety

Products & safety

What we use in your home and how we look after it.

Are your products safe for kids and pets?

We use eco-safe, allergen-aware products and follow label guidance on every surface. If anyone in your home has a sensitivity, tell us and we will adjust.

Can you use my own products?

Of course. Add a note to your booking and leave the products where we can find them.

What if something gets damaged?

It is rare, but if it happens tell us straight away. Our liability insurance covers accidental damage caused during a visit.

What if I am not happy with the result?

Tell us within 24 hours and we will come back and re-clean the areas you are not happy with at no charge. That is our 100% satisfaction guarantee.
