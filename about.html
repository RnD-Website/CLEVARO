Home/About us

Cleaning with care, for homes that matter.

Clevaro started with a simple belief: coming home should feel good. We are a team of trained, insured cleaners who treat every space like our own.

We believe a clean home shouldn’t be complicated. So we built a service that is careful, calm and completely reliable.

Clevaro began in Portland with one cleaner, one van and a very long checklist. Friends told friends, and the checklist stuck: every room, every surface, every visit.

Today we are a team of trained professionals who bring the same careful routine to apartments, family homes and small offices. We use safe products, show up on time and finish with an inspection, so you never have to wonder whether it was done properly.
